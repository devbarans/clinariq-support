Clinariq

On-device clinical statistics

Load your spreadsheet; Clinariq picks the right method, explains results in plain language and builds a publication-ready report. Your data never leaves the device.

Coming soon to the App StoreiPhone, iPad and Mac

Why Clinariq?

Chooses the right method

Normality, variances, expected counts and event numbers decide the method through transparent, reproducible rules, not AI.

Validated computation

At every launch each method is tested against 340 reference cases computed with SciPy, statsmodels and scikit-learn.

Your data stays on the device

No account, cloud storage for research data, advertising or usage analytics. Pro purchases are verified through RevenueCat. Patient data leaves the device only when you share a report.

Research types

  • Compare treatments or patient groups
  • Examine the relationship between two measurements (Pearson, Spearman correlation)
  • Evaluate a diagnostic test or biomarker (ROC)
  • Follow-up, relapse and survival analysis (Kaplan–Meier, Cox)
  • Examine factors affecting an outcome together (multivariable models)

Publication-ready output

Table 1, result tables, Kaplan–Meier, ROC, forest and scatter plots; methods and results text in 10 languages; PDF, Word, Excel, CSV and a reproducibility record with a SHA-256 fingerprint.

Clinariq is a research-only statistics tool. It is not a medical device and must not be used for the diagnosis or treatment of individual patients or for clinical decisions.
