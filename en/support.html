Frequently asked questions

What is included in the free version and Clinariq Pro?

The free version includes one study, on-screen analysis results and the sample size calculator. Clinariq Pro unlocks unlimited studies, multivariable models, Table 1, manuscript text and PDF, Word, Excel and CSV exports. Monthly and annual subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase are available. Paid plans do not include a free trial.

Which files can I open?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV and tab-separated text files with column names in the first row. Title rows, merged cells and total rows are recognised automatically. Files in Mail, WhatsApp or Files can be opened in Clinariq directly from the share menu.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, inside the app's protected container. On iPhone and iPad data files are encrypted and excluded from backups. Research data is never sent to any server; the internet is used only to verify Pro purchases.

How reliable are the calculations?

Each method is tested at every launch against 340 reference cases (2,462 values); a method that fails is not used. See Settings > Statistics engine validation.

Is Clinariq a medical device?

No. Clinariq is for the statistical analysis of research data and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions about individual patients.

How do I delete my data?

Delete studies one by one, or use Settings > "Delete all studies and data". Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Clinariq is a research-only statistics tool. It is not a medical device and must not be used for the diagnosis or treatment of individual patients or for clinical decisions.
