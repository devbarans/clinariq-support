Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how Clinariq, published by Baran Baran, handles your data.

In short

Your research data never leaves your device. The app has no account, advertising, usage analytics or crash reporting. The internet connection is used only to verify Clinariq Pro purchases.

Research data you import

  • The Excel or CSV files you choose are stored only on your device, inside the app's protected container.
  • On iPhone and iPad, data files are encrypted with iOS complete file protection and are excluded from device backups (iCloud or computer backups).
  • Study names, column mappings and analysis results (summary statistics) may be included in your device's own backup. This information belongs to you and is never sent to us.
  • The app flags columns that appear to contain names, national ID numbers, phone numbers or e-mail addresses and never writes these values into any report.

Clinariq Pro purchases

  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  • To verify your purchase and unlock Pro on your devices we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). Only the purchase history (product, date, price, country), a randomly generated anonymous app ID, and the app and operating system versions are sent to it.
  • This information is not linked to your name, e-mail or Apple ID and is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Research data, study names and analysis results are never sent to RevenueCat or to us.
  • RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Sharing

Your data and reports leave the device only when you share or save a file yourself. The privacy terms of the app or service you choose (for example Mail or Files) then apply.

Support

If you e-mail us, we use the information in your message only to answer it. Please do not include patient data in e-mails.

Deleting data

Delete individual studies, or use Settings > "Delete all studies and data". Deleting the app also removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Clinariq is designed for healthcare professionals and researchers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the app and on our website.

Contact

Questions: auniversty@gmail.com

Clinariq is a research-only statistics tool. It is not a medical device and must not be used for the diagnosis or treatment of individual patients or for clinical decisions.
