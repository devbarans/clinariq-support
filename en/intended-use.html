Intended use and limitations

Effective date: 1 October 2026

What Clinariq is

Clinariq is research software that helps physicians and clinical researchers analyse and report their own research data, entirely on their device.

What Clinariq is not

  • Clinariq is not a medical device.
  • It must not be used for the diagnosis, treatment or monitoring of individual patients, or for any clinical decision about a patient.
  • No output of the app is medical advice or a treatment recommendation.
  • Outputs such as ROC cut-off values are research findings for the analysed sample, not cut-offs recommended for clinical use.

Your responsibilities

  • Obtain the ethics approval and participant consent your research requires.
  • Process data in line with GDPR, applicable local law (such as Turkish Law No. 6698) and your institution's rules; use de-identified data wherever possible.
  • Judge whether the study design, the variables and the method selected by the app fit your research question.
  • Have results reviewed by a qualified statistician before publication or presentation.

Accuracy

Every statistical method in the app is tested automatically at each launch against reference values produced with established libraries such as SciPy, statsmodels and scikit-learn; a method that fails is not used. This testing cannot prevent errors caused by incorrect data or an unsuitable study design.

Clinariq is a research-only statistics tool. It is not a medical device and must not be used for the diagnosis or treatment of individual patients or for clinical decisions.
